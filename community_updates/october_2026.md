## Overview

LlamaRisk presents our October 2026 monthly update, summarizing key activities and outlining upcoming priorities.

## Highlights

### Recommendations and inputs

#### Asset onboarding
* [\[Direct-To-AIP\] Onboard PT-AUSD-17DEC2026 to Aave V3 Monad Instance](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-onboard-pt-ausd-17dec2026-to-aave-v3-monad-instance/25701/2) - Supported onboarding given the 76-day maturity horizon and its role as the rollover destination for the prior maturity, which holds 67.4M PT as collateral on Monad, while noting $1.61M pool liquidity seeded shortly before the review.
* [\[Direct-to-AIP\] Onboard PT-USDG-25FEB2027 on X Layer](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-onboard-pt-usdg-25feb2027-on-x-layer/25733/2) - Supported onboarding given the 141-day maturity horizon and its role as the rollover destination for PT-USDG-29OCT2026, which holds 66M PT as collateral. Recommended a new E-Mode that allows existing positions to roll over without closing. The pool held $1M in liquidity, and looping depends on external OKX incentives, as the 2.79% implied yield sits below most stablecoin borrow rates on X Layer.
* [\[Direct-to-AIP\] Asset Listing - USDe X Layer](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-asset-listing-usde-x-layer/25688/4) - Supported onboarding contingent on the team bootstrapping $5M in DEX liquidity before launch, as no local exit venue for liquidators existed at the time of review. Flagged that `setPeer` can bypass the OFT's 24-hour timelock via the Ethena multisig and that timelock coverage remains absent on Solana and TON, the two largest USDe balances outside Ethereum. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/ethena-usde-on-aave-x-layer-assessments/25827/2).
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard PAXGy to Aave V4 Global Dollar Hub](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-paxgy-to-aave-v4-global-dollar-hub/25499/3) - Supported onboarding on the Gold Spoke against USDG at a 70% collateral factor and on a new Gold Correlated Spoke against PAXG at 85%, conditional on the $3M DEX liquidity committed by Paxos Labs being live at listing. Contracts sit behind a 24-hour timelock, with the main risk lying in the off-chain gold-lending book, which targets about 80% of the vault across four borrowers and is valued from Paxos Labs' own statements without an independent check, and insurance does not cover a borrower default. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/paxgy-on-aave-ethereum-assessments/25831/2).
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard USAT to Aave V3 Core Instance and Aave V4 Core Hub on Ethereum](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usat-to-aave-v3-core-instance-and-aave-v4-core-hub-on-ethereum/25817/2) - Supported onboarding the Anchorage-issued stablecoin as a borrowable, non-collateral reserve priced at a hardcoded $1, so that transient depegs cannot trigger liquidations. Upgrades and all privileged functions sit with a 3-of-5 multisig without a timelock or pause function, and adding a timelock with role separation is required before the listing is widened. A robust Chainlink feed is required for enabling USAT as collateral. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/tether-america-usd-usat-on-aave-ethereum-assessments/25832/2).
* [\[Direct-to-AIP\] Asset Listing - SPCXc to the Aave V4 Equities Hub on Base](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-asset-listing-spcxc-to-the-aave-v4-equities-hub-on-base/25850/2) - Supported onboarding SpaceX stock in Coinbase B20 form with a 55% collateral factor, 6.5% maximum liquidation bonus, and 10% collateral risk set more conservatively than the Magnificent Seven. The stricter terms reflect its four-month trading history, 81% annualized volatility, and lock-up releases equal to 84% of the current float between October 26 and December 8, with parameters to be reviewed monthly while the listing history remains short. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/coinbase-b20-equities-on-base-assessments/25690/3).
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard Native BTC as Collateral to Aave V4 via Babylon Trustless Bitcoin Vaults](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-native-btc-as-collateral-to-aave-v4-via-babylon-trustless-bitcoin-vaults/25845/10) - Supported onboarding native BTC as non-borrowable collateral with a 78% collateral factor. Noted that the contracts were not yet deployed on Ethereum and that initial audits identified several critical and high-severity issues, all reported as resolved, with subsequent audit rounds finding no further critical issues. Also highlighted timelock protections for contract upgrades and parameter changes, an active $500K Sherlock bug bounty, and recommended an independent Proof of Reserves for BTC held in the vaults. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/babylon-native-btc-on-aave-ethereum-assessments/25907/2).
* [\[Direct-to-AIP\] Asset Listing - USDG Arbitrum](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-asset-listing-usdg-arbitrum/25811/2) - Supported onboarding as a borrowable, non-collateral asset. Token upgrades sit behind a 24-hour timelock, though one Paxos EOA can change mint limits, peers, rate limits, and the bridge wrapper without delay, and we recommend moving these powers behind a timelock. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/global-dollar-usdg-on-aave-arbitrum-assessments/25912/2).
* [\[Direct to AIP\] Onboard USAT to Aave V4 Core Instance on Avalanche](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-onboard-usat-to-aave-v4-core-instance-on-avalanche/25880/2) - Supported onboarding as a borrow-only reserve, contingent on bootstrapping $5M in DEX liquidity on Avalanche. Noted that USAT had no supply or trading venue on Avalanche at the time of review. Key risks include uncapped owner minting and a bridge route without rate limits, with implementation of a rate limit requested within one month of onboarding. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/tether-america-usd-usat-on-aave-avalanche-assessments/25927/2).

### Research and analysis
* [\[Direct-to-AIP\] CAPO SnapshotRatio Update Across Aave V3: October 2026](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-capo-snapshotratio-update-across-aave-v3-october-2026/25837) - Proposed refreshing the `snapshotRatio` for 27 reserves across 11 Aave V3 instances to address stale snapshots and the growing spread between the CAPO upper bound and current ratios. Reserves were selected using a deficit-trigger condition, covering those where an inflation attack could leave positions undercollateralized today or within the next 30 days based on current APY trends. The widest spread identified was 16.76% on wstETH on Optimism, where the snapshot was 971 days old.
* [JAAA Reserve Parameter Update on Aave V3 Horizon](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-jaaa-reserve-parameter-update-on-aave-v3-horizon-2026-10-15/25854) - Proposed raising the JAAA reserve-level LTV from 81% to 87% and LT from 86% to 89% following the fund's settlement record, with 91 of 93 redemption orders totaling $717.3M over the past year paid within one business day and the remaining two at T+2. The parameters are sized to a three-business-day exit window, with the resulting 6.55% solvency margin covering the worst CLO senior index decline over any window of up to six trading days (5.99%).

### Incident response
* [Binance Hot Wallet Exploit: Aave's Response](https://governance.aave.com/t/binance-hot-wallet-exploit-aaves-response/25954) - Provided details on the October 29 exploit and the precautionary freeze of 175 reserves on Aave V3 and 117 on Aave V4, prepared with Aave Labs and executed by the guardian multisigs, with Horizon left open as its collateral is permissioned. The exploiter's only Aave position, 60M USDT supplied against 19,762 WETH borrowed, remained over-collateralized and subject to normal liquidation. A [follow-up update](https://governance.aave.com/t/binance-hot-wallet-exploit-aaves-response/25954/18) covered the four-wave unfreeze plan, under which assets held across the exploiter's addresses, including USDT, USDC, and USDT0, stay at LTV 0, along with Risk Steward actions:
    * [Temporary Slope2 Reductions](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-temporary-slope2-reductions-on-aave-v3-and-aave-v4-2026-10-30/25955) - Limited the borrow rate at full utilization to 10% APR, as withdrawals pushed frozen reserves to 92-100% utilization while new supply was blocked. Slope2 was [restored](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-slope2-increases-on-aave-v3-and-aave-v4-2026-10-31/25970) once reserves were unfrozen.
    * [Borrow and Draw Cap Reductions](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-borrow-and-draw-cap-reductions-on-aave-v3-and-aave-v4-2026-10-30/25960) - Reduced caps on the largest borrow paths to about 2% above pre-exploit borrowing, removing about $3.7B of unused capacity on Aave V3 and $130M on Aave V4 that would otherwise have become available at once on reopening.
* [tBTC Mint & Redeem Pause: Aave's Response](https://governance.aave.com/t/tbtc-mint-redeem-pause-aaves-response/25965) - Recommended a pre-emptive freeze of tBTC on Aave V3 Ethereum Core following Threshold Network's pause of mints and redeems, to prevent exposure from growing until the incident is confirmed to be mitigated. Ethereum Core was the only market where tBTC could still support new borrowing, as the Base and Arbitrum reserves were already frozen. tBTC keeps its liquidation threshold, so health factors of existing positions do not change.
