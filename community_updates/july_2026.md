# LlamaRisk - Monthly Community Update 

# July 2026

## Overview

LlamaRisk presents our July 2026 monthly update, summarizing key activities and outlining upcoming priorities.

## Highlights

### Recommendations and inputs

#### Asset onboarding
* [\[Direct to AIP\] Onboard syrupUSDG on Aave V4 Global Dollar Hub](https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-onboard-syrupusdg-on-aave-v4-global-dollar-hub/25281/2) - Supported onboarding contingent on sufficient DEX liquidity bootstrapping, which the team implemented shortly. A full risk assessment was [provided separately](https://governance.aave.com/t/maple-syrup-usdg-syrupusdg-on-aave-ethereum/25327/2).
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard USDai & sUSDai to Aave V3 Arbitrum Instance](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260/10) - Provided an updated assessment supporting onboarding, with the completed transition to PYUSD backing, KYC-restricted minting and redemption, while noting deterioration in USDai secondary market liquidity.
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard syrupUSDC to Aave V3 Core Instance](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-syrupusdc-to-aave-v3-core-instance/22456/15)- Provided an updated assessment supporting the onboarding with improvements observed in redemption times, six additional audits, introduction of a 24-hour timelock on contract upgrades, and stable DEX liquidity.
* [\[ARFC\] Onboard PT-AUSD-8OCT2026 to Aave V3 Monad Instance](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-pt-ausd-8oct2026-to-aave-v3-monad-instance/25331/2) - Supported onboarding given the long maturity horizon justifying integration efforts, while noting the limited $5.7M AMM liquidity and recommending an updated E-Mode borrowable set comprising USDT0, USDC, GHO, and USDe.

#### GHO related
* [\[ARFC\] Launch sGHO Cross-Chain](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-launch-sgho-cross-chain/25217/2) - Supported the launch of cross-chain sGHO, enabling L2 access via Chainlink CCIP bridging while preserving unified yield generation and accounting through a single ERC-4626 vault.

#### Misc.
* [\[ARFC\] SVR Expansion: Next Phase of Multi-Network Expansion](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-svr-expansion-next-phase-of-multi-network-expansion/25332/2) - Supported expanding SVR to Avalanche, Polygon, and BNB Chain, leveraging Chainlink’s Atlas auction infrastructure already used on Base and Arbitrum, with a 10-second fallback delay.

### Research and analysis
* [\[ARFC\] Oracle Deprecation for Long-tail Assets Across Aave V2 and V3](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-oracle-deprecation-for-long-tail-assets-across-aave-v2-and-v3/25400) - Published a proposal to deprecate Chainlink price feeds for selected long-tail assets classified as high operational risk due to low adoption and illiquidity, recommending asset-specific migration plans using fixed-price adapters and controlled parameters to facilitate an orderly wind-down.
* [\[ARFC\] Low Adoption Asset Deprecation on Aave V3](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-low-adoption-asset-deprecation-on-aave-v3/25401) - Published a comprehensive deprecation proposal to reduce Aave’s risk surface, covering 50 reserves and 21 matured Pendle PTs ($85.3M supply, $11.5M debt) alongside the wind-down of six low-adoption markets (Sonic, Scroll, zkSync, Metis, Soneium, and Aptos; $12.8M supply, $4.1M debt). Coordinated with the companion Oracle Deprecation ARFC, applying reserve freezes, cap reductions to 1, and fixed-price oracles for affected assets

### Community Engagement

## Upcoming Focus Areas
* **Aave V4 Growth**: Monitor V4 adoption and propose further add and draw cap increases across Ethereum and Avalanche, alongside ongoing liquidation parameter tuning.
* **SVR Support**: Expand SVR analytics to support upcoming deployments on Avalanche, Polygon, and BNB Chain.
* **RWA Assessments**: Continue due diligence for new assets and provide ongoing risk support across the Horizon RWA pipeline.
* **Bridge Compliance**: Continue working with bridge providers and asset issuers to advance a standard methodology for bridge rate limits.
* [Risk Stewards](https://dashboard.llamarisk.com/protocols/aave/risk-parameter-updates): Maintain day-to-day risk parameter operations across Aave V3 and V4.

We welcome community feedback and suggestions. Please share any questions, ideas, or areas where you would like the LlamaRisk team to focus more.
